Prior to joining AUEB, I was a post-doctoral researcher in the [Archimedes](https://archimedesai.gr/en/) research unit of the [Athena](https://www.athenarc.gr/en) research center, working with [Panayotis Mertikopoulos](https://polaris.imag.fr/panayotis.mertikopoulos/).
From December 2019 to December 2023, I was a post-doctoral researcher at the [MaLGa](https://malga.unige.it/) research center, working with [Silvia Villa](http://www.dima.unige.it/~villa/) and [Lorenzo Rosasco](http://web.mit.edu/lrosasco/www/).

I completed my Ph.D. at the [Institut de Mathématiques de Bordeaux](https://www.math.u-bordeaux.fr/imb/), under the supervision of [Charles Dossal](https://perso.math.univ-toulouse.fr/cdossal/) and [Jean-François Aujol](https://www.math.u-bordeaux.fr/~jaujol/). Before that, I completed my master's studies at Université Claude Bernard Lyon 1 and École normale supérieure de Lyon, and my undergraduate studies in the Department of Mathematics of Aristotle University of Thessaloniki in Greece.
